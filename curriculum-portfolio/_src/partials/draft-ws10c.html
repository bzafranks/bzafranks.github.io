Feedback on Your Opinion Essay: "Longer Recess"

Great job on this essay! You stated your opinion clearly in the first sentence, and you kept it going all the way to the end.

What You Did Well

  • Your opinion is clear from the start. Your first sentence tells the reader exactly what you think.
  • You used strong details. Meeting Maya, the ball bin, and the fourth graders getting 20 minutes all make your ideas feel real.
  • You used transition words. "First," "Second," and "Also" help the reader follow your reasons.
  • You answered the other side. Mentioning that some people think recess is a waste of time shows you thought about different views.
  • Your ending is strong. You restated your opinion and finished with a request that makes the reader want to agree.

Scores

Reasons and evidence: 3 out of 4 You gave three reasons and backed some of them with details, like the wiggly feeling after lunch and Maya. Your first reason, exercise, needs more explanation. Your last claim, that we will learn more if we focus better, is not yet supported with an example.

Conventions: 3 out of 4 Your sentences are clear and mostly correct, but you have a few errors that repeat. Fixing them would be an easy way to raise this score.

Your Next Steps

  1. Explain your exercise reason more. Right now you say, "When we run around at recess we get energy out. Then we can focus better in class." Try adding one more sentence that explains how that happens. For example: "When my body has been moving, I sit still more easily and can concentrate on my math worksheet instead of fidgeting."

  2. Fix the spelling and grammar errors that repeat. Check these first:

  3. "would not of happened" → "would not have happened"
  4. "Its almost time" and "Its not fair" → "It's almost time" and "It's not fair" (it's means "it is")
  5. "it is to short" → "it is too short"

  6. "Second recess is when" → "Second, recess is when" (add a comma after an introductory word)

  7. Support your last reason. You wrote, "if we focus better we will learn more anyway." Add an example of how focusing helps learning. For instance, you could describe a time when you understood a lesson better after a break. Then the reader will believe your claim.

Keep up the good work. Your ideas are clear and convincing, and with a few more details and some careful proofreading, this essay could be a 4 in every category!
