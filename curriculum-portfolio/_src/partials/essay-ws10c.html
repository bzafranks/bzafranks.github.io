Longer Recess

I think our school should have a longer recess. Recess is only 15 minutes and that is not enough time. Some people think recess is a waste of time but I disagree.

First, kids need exercise. When we run around at recess we get energy out. Then we can focus better in class. My teacher says we are wiggly after lunch. If we had more recess we would not be so wiggly.

Second recess is when we make friends. In class we have to be quiet and work. At recess you can talk to anybody and play games like tag and four square. Last week I met a new girl named Maya and now she is my friend. That would not of happened in class.

Also, 15 minutes goes by really fast. By the time you get your ball out of the bin, its almost time to line up. Its not fair because the fourth graders get 20 minutes.

Some people say we will lose learning time. But if we focus better we will learn more anyway.

In conclusion, recess should be longer. It helps us get exercise, make friends, and it is to short now. Please make recess longer!
