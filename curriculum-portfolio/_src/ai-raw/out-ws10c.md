# Feedback on Your Opinion Essay: "Longer Recess"

## Scores

**Opinion and focus: 4 out of 4**
Your opinion is clear, and every paragraph connects to it. Nothing in the essay wanders away from the topic.

**Reasons and evidence: 3 out of 4**
You gave three reasons and backed some of them with details, like the wiggly feeling after lunch and Maya. Your first reason, exercise, needs more explanation. Your last claim, that we will learn more if we focus better, is not yet supported with an example.

**Organization: 3 out of 4**
Your essay has an introduction, body paragraphs, a counterargument, and a conclusion. The order works. The "Also" paragraph about the short time and the "Some people say" paragraph are a little short compared to the others, so they feel less developed.

**Conventions: 3 out of 4**
Your sentences are clear and mostly correct, but you have a few errors that repeat. Fixing them would be an easy way to raise this score.

## Your Next Steps

1. **Explain your exercise reason more.** Right now you say, "When we run around at recess we get energy out. Then we can focus better in class." Try adding one more sentence that explains *how* that happens. For example: *"When my body has been moving, I sit still more easily and can concentrate on my math worksheet instead of fidgeting."*

2. **Fix the spelling and grammar errors that repeat.** Check these first:
   - "would not **of** happened" → "would not **have** happened"
   - "**Its** almost time" and "**Its** not fair" → "**It's** almost time" and "**It's** not fair" (*it's* means "it is")
   - "it is **to** short" → "it is **too** short"
   - "**Second** recess is when" → "**Second,** recess is when" (add a comma after an introductory word)

3. **Support your last reason.** You wrote, "if we focus better we will learn more anyway." Add an example of how focusing helps learning. For instance, you could describe a time when you understood a lesson better after a break. Then the reader will believe your claim.

Keep up the good work. Your ideas are clear and convincing, and with a few more details and some careful proofreading, this essay could be a 4 in every category!
