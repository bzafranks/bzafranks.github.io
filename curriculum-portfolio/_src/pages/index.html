How I use AI

I use AI tools for drafting, research leads and checking, never as the final word. In WS7 and WS10, AI output is the thing being reviewed, so its errors are real and shown as generated. Everywhere else I set the design, wrote or rewrote the content, and checked every fact, standard and reference myself. No sample claims results with learners, because none has been used with learners yet.
